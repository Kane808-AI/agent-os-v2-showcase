# Etsy Digital Listing Guardrails

**Status:** Candidate. Research use only. This is not current platform truth or
an approved procedure. Etsy rules, listing fields, disclosures, and account
state require verification from official sources and the live tenant before
execution.

## Scope

These guardrails apply to Northwind reference-pack research for digital products,
including the Etsy shop concepts. They do not authorize publishing, advertising,
price changes, or account modification.

## Durable lessons

- Classify the product as digital, physical, or print-on-demand before creating
  copy. Digital listings must not inherit shipping or production-partner
  language from physical-product templates.
- Preserve lineage between the product source, permitted generative inputs,
  exported files, preview images, copy, and customer instructions.
- Make the delivery format and any material AI assistance clear and truthful.
- Treat asset QA as a release gate: inspect the actual customer files, verify
  previews against them, and confirm filenames, dimensions, links, and
  instructions.
- Draft first. A separately authorized publisher must inspect the final draft
  and read back the resulting live listing before completion is claimed.
- Validate one complete listing and its customer journey before scaling a
  template across a catalog.

## Deliberately excluded

No current tag limit, fee, policy quotation, marketplace benchmark, browser
instruction, agent name, file path, or account configuration was imported.

## Promotion checklist

Promotion requires current official Etsy policy evidence, live-field
validation, a rights and disclosure review, an end-to-end test listing, a
rollback plan, and an authorized human reviewer.
