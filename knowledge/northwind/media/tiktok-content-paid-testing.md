# TikTok Content and Paid Testing

**Status:** Candidate. Research use only. This record is not current TikTok
product, advertising, or policy truth and is not an approved media-buying
procedure.

## Scope

This candidate strategy supports Northwind personal-brand, client-acquisition,
affiliate, and content research. It does not authorize posting, connecting an
account, creating an audience, or spending money.

## Durable lessons

- Separate organic publishing, in-app promotion, native advertising, and
  creator-authorized formats in the experiment design. Current availability
  and requirements must be verified in official product documentation.
- Give each test a hypothesis, baseline, target audience, creative premise,
  destination, tracking plan, measurement window, loss limit, and decision
  rule.
- Use organic response as one input to creative selection, not as proof that a
  paid placement will convert.
- Optimize for the relevant downstream outcome—qualified lead, sale,
  contribution margin, or retained audience—not raw views.
- Test creative and offer variables deliberately. Do not boost every post or
  change several causal variables without recording the change.
- Attribute outcomes through verified events and independent business data
  when possible. A platform-reported action is evidence, not final truth.

## Safety boundaries

Do not buy followers or engagement, scrape restricted data, automate deceptive
interaction, conceal sponsorship, or bypass consent and platform controls.

## Promotion checklist

Promotion requires current official policy and feature verification, validated
tracking, approved creative and disclosures, a tenant-specific spend envelope,
stop conditions, finance approval where required, and an independent readback
of the launched campaign.
