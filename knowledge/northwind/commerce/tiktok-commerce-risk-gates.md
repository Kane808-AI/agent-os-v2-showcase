# TikTok Commerce Risk Gates

**Status:** Candidate. Research use only. This record is not an approved launch
procedure and does not state current fees, service levels, eligibility, or
platform policy.

## Scope

These gates support Northwind research into seller and creator-affiliate commerce
models. They do not authorize enrollment, product listing, vendor selection,
inventory commitment, advertising, or fulfillment.

## Pre-launch gates

- Identify whether the business is acting as seller, creator affiliate, or
  another current platform role. Keep account ownership and economics explicit.
- Model revenue after product cost, platform and payment charges, commission,
  acquisition cost, shipping, returns, taxes, support, and fulfillment failure.
- Verify product rights, claims, identity, tax, know-your-customer,
  jurisdiction, and disclosure requirements using current authoritative
  sources.
- Validate supplier evidence, inventory truth, delivery capability, return
  handling, customer support, and escalation ownership.
- Run a bounded manual pilot and a test order before automating catalog,
  promotion, or fulfillment activity.
- Keep each tenant and legal entity isolated. Resolve live identifiers at
  execution time and read back the created state.

## Deliberately excluded

No historical fee percentage, delivery deadline, vendor recommendation, tool
claim, eligibility rule, or account state was imported.

## Promotion checklist

Promotion requires verified current seller terms, complete unit economics,
test-order evidence, an approved loss limit, support and returns readiness,
security and privacy review, and business-owner approval.
