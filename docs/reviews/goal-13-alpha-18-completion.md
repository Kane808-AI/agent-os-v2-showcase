**Decision:** GO to begin Goal 14

**Date:** 2026-07-31

**Release:** `2.0.0-alpha.18`

## Exit-criteria result

Goal 13 is complete. The platform now provides:

- one versioned, business-neutral, non-executing capability-pack policy;
- exact required coverage and deterministic acceptance for 13 packs spanning
  digital marketing/consulting with SEO/GEO, YouTube, applications, physical
  products, commerce, Finance, Accounting, Sales, Operations, Customer Success,
  Research, Engineering, and QA;
- explicit owner, independent verifier, metrics, read-only sources, evidence,
  outputs, allowed modes/actions, prohibitions, and happy/boundary fixtures for
  every pack;
- an inert Northwind mapping across services, commerce, media, and ventures;
- append-only, idempotent acceptance evidence bound to exact pack and evaluator
  hashes;
- privacy-safe aggregate performance import with ordered funnel arithmetic,
  sample floors, same-scope evidence, independent QA, and non-causality labels;
- strict isolation between aggregate evidence and Goal 12 event-level
  attribution or candidate learning;
- dashboard-first communications with replaceable Slack, Telegram, Discord,
  Teams, and email proposal descriptors and no send operation; and
- dashboard, schema attestation, doctor recomputation, adversarial forgery, and
  verified-backup coverage for Goal 13 evidence.

No pack, account, provider, credential, channel, schedule, publisher, contact
adapter, ad buyer, financial executor, or production writer is activated.

## Preserved controls

Goal 13 consumes the existing role registry and requires independent verifier
identity; it does not grant tool access through a pack. Tenant/business evidence
scope is checked before aggregate import and at durable attestation. Finance and
Accounting remain separate, money movement stays globally prohibited, and no
pack can bypass current authority, approval, spend, emergency-stop, or execution
truth controls.

Goal 10 remains the only routing service. Goal 11 remains the only real-model
shadow adapter boundary, with exact credential isolation, structured validation,
usage telemetry, and explicit fallback. Goal 12 retains its selected-offer,
content, click-before-conversion, independent QA, and candidate-learning gates.

## Bounded risk accepted

Capability acceptance proves contract completeness and deterministic boundary
behavior, not production performance. Aggregate reports can contain upstream
platform error, reporting delay, selection bias, and correlation that does not
generalize. Verification deliberately makes no causal or individual-attribution
claim.

Goal 14 must qualify real adapters and deployments: tenant packaging,
onboarding, least-privilege secrets, observability, disaster recovery, hostile
administrator and crash consistency review, cost model, upgrades, and
capability-by-capability legacy cutover with rollback. No Goal 13 artifact is
authority to skip that production gate.
