# Etsy Product Strategy Candidate

**Status:** Candidate. Research use only. This is a hypothesis, not an approved
commercial strategy and not current marketplace truth.

## Scope

This record supports research for Northwind reference businesses evaluating
digital-product demand on Etsy. It does not authorize product launch, pricing,
advertising, or spend.

## Research hypothesis

Product development should begin with a narrow customer problem and a
measurable buying occasion rather than a large undifferentiated catalog. Demand
signals should be triangulated across current search behavior, competitive
offers, customer language, and small controlled tests. Creative quality alone
is not evidence of product-market fit.

Each experiment should define the audience, offer, differentiator, acquisition
route, expected customer action, measurement window, maximum loss, and stop or
iterate rule. Organic distribution and paid distribution are separate tests;
neither should be treated as a substitute for conversion evidence.

Catalog expansion should follow validated delivery quality and a meaningful
downstream outcome such as qualified traffic, conversion, contribution margin,
or repeat purchase. Views, impressions, and listing count are diagnostic
signals, not revenue proof.

## Deliberately excluded

Historical prices, demand estimates, keyword volumes, competitor counts,
platform features, and forecasted sales were not migrated as facts.

## Promotion checklist

Promotion requires fresh marketplace research, verified unit economics,
rights-safe product inputs, an approved testing budget, instrumented
attribution, explicit loss limits, and review by the business owner and finance
authority.
